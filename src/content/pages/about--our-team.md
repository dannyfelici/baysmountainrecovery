At Bays Mountain Recovery in Kingsport, Tennessee, we believe meaningful recovery begins with human connection. [Our approach](/about/) to addiction treatment reflects that philosophy, bringing together medical care, therapeutic support, and individualized attention in a smaller, more personal setting.

## A Smaller Setting Where Relationships Matter

Our 25-bed inpatient facility provides medical detox and residential addiction treatment in one location. Unlike outpatient programs, where patients return home between appointments, adults in our care live at the facility while receiving treatment.

This creates opportunities for patients and care professionals to become familiar with one another over time. As you move through treatment, the people supporting your recovery can gain a deeper understanding of your experiences, challenges, strengths, and goals.

We believe these relationships matter. Feeling comfortable asking questions, discussing difficult emotions, and expressing your concerns can help you participate more fully in your own recovery.

At Bays Mountain Recovery, our commitment to personal care begins with recognizing that you're more than your addiction. You're an individual with a unique history, personal relationships, and hopes for the future.

## Medical and Clinical Professionals Working Together

Effective addiction treatment involves addressing physical health, emotional well-being, and the behaviors associated with substance use. Diverse professionals contribute their knowledge and experience to support these interconnected needs.

Our treatment model brings together complementary areas of care:

* **Medical care and withdrawal management**: Medically trained professionals help assess physical health, monitor withdrawal symptoms, and support stabilization during detox.
* **Licensed behavioral health clinicians**: Qualified therapists and addiction treatment professionals help patients understand substance use, develop coping strategies, and work toward their recovery goals.
* **Co-occurring mental health support**: Addiction treatment considers the relationship between substance use and conditions such as anxiety, depression, and trauma-related symptoms.
* **Recovery planning and continued support**: Treatment professionals help patients prepare for the challenges of recovery, develop healthier routines, and identify appropriate next steps.

Our [medical detox](/detox/) and [residential treatment](/residential-treatment/) programs are available at the same Kingsport facility, allowing adults who need both levels of care to continue their treatment in a familiar environment.

## Meet the People Behind Your Care

Professional qualifications are important, but so are the personal qualities that help patients feel understood and supported. We believe effective addiction treatment requires clinical knowledge, compassion, clear communication, and respect for each person's experiences.

Our commitment is to create an environment where adults seeking recovery feel comfortable asking for help, participating in treatment, and developing meaningful connections with the people around them.
