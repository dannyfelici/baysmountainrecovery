Located in Kingsport, Tennessee, Bays Mountain Recovery is dedicated to helping adults overcome substance use disorders and address co-occurring mental health conditions. Our approach brings together medical detox and residential addiction treatment, creating a connected path toward recovery.

We're proud to be part of East Tennessee, and our commitment extends beyond the individuals who seek treatment. We recognize that healthier individuals can help build stronger families, and stronger families help create healthier communities.

## Our Approach to Addiction Treatment

We believe meaningful recovery begins when people feel heard, respected, and supported. Addiction is a complex condition that affects everyone differently, and treatment should recognize the unique experiences, challenges, and strengths of each person.

Our approach to care is grounded in three important principles.

### Compassion and Respect

Asking for help with addiction can be difficult. You may feel ashamed of past decisions, worried about how others see you, or uncertain about what treatment will involve.

We believe you deserve compassion, not judgment.

At Bays Mountain Recovery, our commitment is to meet you where you are in your recovery journey. Whether you're seeking treatment for the first time or returning after a setback, your experiences matter. You deserve to be treated as a person with individual needs, goals, and hopes for the future, not defined by your substance use.

### Care Centered on Your Individual Needs

No two people experience addiction in the same way; there is no one-size-fits-all approach to treatment. Your substance use history, physical and mental health, relationships, personal experiences, and living circumstances can all influence your recovery.

That's why we believe addiction treatment should begin with understanding the individual.

Through assessment and individualized treatment planning, the goal is to identify your needs and develop an approach that supports meaningful progress. Treatment should recognize your strengths while helping you address the challenges that may be standing in the way of recovery.

### Evidence-Based Treatment and Whole-Person Recovery

Overcoming addiction involves more than stopping alcohol or drug use. Recovery also means understanding the thoughts, emotions, behaviors, and experiences that may contribute to substance use.

Our treatment philosophy emphasizes evidence-based therapeutic approaches that help people develop healthier coping skills, manage cravings, recognize triggers, and prepare for life after treatment.

We also recognize the importance of physical health, emotional well-being, and healthy daily routines. By addressing addiction as part of a person's overall well-being, treatment can help build a stronger foundation for lasting recovery.

## Connected Care From Medical Detox to Residential Treatment

For many people, addiction recovery begins with withdrawal. Depending on the substance involved, this process can be uncomfortable, emotionally challenging, or medically dangerous.

Bays Mountain Recovery is designed to provide two connected levels of care at our Kingsport facility.

1. Our [medical detox program](/detox/) focuses on managing withdrawal symptoms, monitoring physical health, and helping patients stabilize after stopping drugs or alcohol.
2. Once withdrawal is managed, patients who need continued support may transition into [residential treatment](/residential-treatment/), where they can begin addressing the emotional, behavioral, and psychological challenges associated with addiction.

Having both levels of care available at one facility helps reduce unnecessary disruption during a vulnerable time. Instead of completing detox and searching for another treatment provider, patients who are clinically appropriate for residential care can continue their recovery in a familiar setting.

This connected approach reflects an important part of our philosophy: recovery is a process, and every stage deserves thoughtful, coordinated support.

## Supporting Individuals, Strengthening Families and Communities

Addiction rarely affects just one person. Its effects can extend into relationships, families, workplaces, and entire communities.

Behind every person struggling with substance use are people who may be worried, confused, frustrated, or hoping for change. Parents worry about their children. Spouses and partners struggle to rebuild trust. Children may feel the effects of a loved one's addiction without fully understanding what's happening.

We believe these relationships matter.

When someone begins recovery, they're not simply working toward a life without drugs or alcohol. They're also creating opportunities to reconnect with loved ones, rebuild trust, take on responsibilities, and become more engaged in the world around them.

These changes take time, and recovery doesn't automatically repair every relationship. But meaningful progress can create possibilities for healing that extend beyond the individual.

At Bays Mountain Recovery, we understand that every person seeking treatment is also part of a family, a network of relationships, and a larger community. Our commitment to addiction recovery is also a commitment to the families and communities of East Tennessee.

### Our Commitment to East Tennessee

East Tennessee is a place where family, community, and connection hold deep meaning. From the neighborhoods of Kingsport to the surrounding Tri-Cities and Appalachian communities, the region is home to people whose lives and experiences deserve understanding and respect.

Substance use disorders affect people throughout these communities, regardless of their age, background, occupation, or circumstances.

Bays Mountain Recovery is committed to being a resource for adults seeking addiction treatment in Northeast Tennessee. Our goal is to provide compassionate, individualized care while respecting the relationships, responsibilities, and communities that are part of each person's life.

We believe people shouldn't have to leave behind their dignity or sense of belonging when they ask for help. Recovery should offer opportunities to rediscover those connections and build a healthier future.

## Sober and Family-Friendly Activities in Kingsport, TN

Recovery also involves rediscovering the everyday experiences that make life meaningful. Spending time outdoors, enjoying shared activities, and reconnecting with family and friends can help support a healthier, more balanced lifestyle.

Kingsport offers a variety of alcohol-free recreational opportunities that encourage connection, relaxation, and time together.

* [**Bays Mountain Park & Planetarium**](https://www.baysmountain.com/): Explore scenic hiking trails, wildlife habitats, a beautiful lake, and educational planetarium programs. The park offers opportunities to enjoy nature, exercise, and spend time with family.
* [**Warriors' Path State Park**](https://tnstateparks.com/parks/warriors-path): Located along Patrick Henry Reservoir, this state park offers walking trails, picnic areas, playgrounds, and outdoor recreation. It's a welcoming place to enjoy fresh air and time with loved ones.
* [**Kingsport Carousel**](https://visitkingsport.com/explore/kingsport-carousel/): This community-built attraction features beautifully hand-carved animals and artwork celebrating Kingsport's history. It's a fun, family-friendly destination that reflects the creativity and spirit of the community.
* [**Kingsport Greenbelt**](https://www.kingsportgreenbelt.com/): This scenic paved trail winds through parks, neighborhoods, and natural areas. It's ideal for walking, biking, or enjoying a peaceful afternoon outdoors.

These attractions are community resources, not activities provided as part of Bays Mountain Recovery's residential treatment program. They provide healthy experiences and connections people can enjoy as they build a life in recovery.
