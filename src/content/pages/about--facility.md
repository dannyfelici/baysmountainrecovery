At Bays Mountain Recovery, our 25-bed inpatient addiction treatment facility in Kingsport, Tennessee, is designed around a smaller, more personal approach to recovery. Here, compassionate care and meaningful human connections are central to our philosophy, helping you focus on your health and the opportunities ahead.

Set amid the wooded ridges and rolling valleys of Northeast Tennessee, our facility reflects our belief that recovery should offer an opportunity to slow down, find stability, and begin again.

## A Smaller Setting for a More Personal Recovery Experience

Entering addiction treatment can feel overwhelming, especially when you're uncertain about what to expect. At Bays Mountain Recovery, we believe recovery begins with treating you as a person, not defining you by your addiction.

Our 25-bed facility offers a more intimate residential setting where your experiences, concerns, and recovery goals matter. Rather than expecting everyone to follow the same path, our approach emphasizes individualized care, compassion, and respect.

We believe a supportive treatment environment should help you:

* **Feel welcomed and respected**. Your experiences and circumstances deserve understanding, not judgment.
* **Build meaningful connections**. Sharing experiences and receiving encouragement from others can help reduce the isolation often associated with addiction.
* **Focus on personal growth**. Stepping away from everyday responsibilities provides an opportunity to establish healthier routines and develop recovery skills.
* **Prepare for life beyond treatment**. Recovery involves building confidence, strengthening relationships, and developing a foundation for continued well-being.

Our philosophy is simple: meaningful change becomes possible when people feel supported, understood, and empowered to take the next step.

## Medical Detox and Residential Treatment in One Location

Bays Mountain Recovery provides two connected levels of inpatient addiction care at our Kingsport facility. This allows adults who need both services to move from withdrawal stabilization into continued treatment without changing locations.

### Medical Detox

Our [medical detox program](/detox/) helps adults manage withdrawal from alcohol and other substances while addressing immediate physical and emotional needs. Medical assessment, monitoring, and withdrawal management provide a foundation for continued care when appropriate.

### Residential Addiction Treatment

Our [residential treatment program](/residential-treatment/) provides a structured, live-in environment where you can focus on understanding your addiction, developing healthier coping strategies, and preparing for continued recovery.

Having both programs in one location helps create continuity between the early stages of treatment and the ongoing work of recovery.

## Recovery in the Foothills of the Southern Appalachians

Kingsport is nestled in the Ridge-and-Valley region of the Southern Appalachian Mountains, where forested ridges rise above winding waterways and green valleys. The changing seasons bring lush spring landscapes, rich summer greenery, brilliant autumn colors, and quiet winter mornings.

Nearby Bays Mountain is one of the area's most recognizable natural landmarks. The surrounding region is home to Bays Mountain Park, the Kingsport Greenbelt, and the Holston River, reflecting the close connection between this East Tennessee community and its natural environment.

There's something reassuring about the slower rhythms of the Appalachian foothills. The mountains and valleys offer a reminder that change takes time and that even difficult seasons can give way to new beginnings.

Research suggests that spending time in natural environments may help reduce stress and support emotional well-being <sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>. While a peaceful setting is no substitute for professional addiction treatment, we believe the character of Northeast Tennessee provides a fitting backdrop for reflection, renewed purpose, and personal growth.

## Close to Home, Connected to Our Community

For many people, seeking treatment close to home can make an unfamiliar experience feel more manageable. Bays Mountain Recovery serves adults throughout Kingsport, Johnson City, Bristol, Greeneville, Church Hill, and the surrounding Tri-Cities region, while welcoming individuals from elsewhere in Tennessee.

Our connection to East Tennessee reflects a larger commitment to helping people rebuild their lives and relationships. We believe healthier individuals help strengthen families, and stronger families contribute to healthier communities.
